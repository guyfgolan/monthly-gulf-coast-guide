1 · Amendment 3 is polling short of 60%

Early voting is underway for the Nov 3 ballot. Amendment 3 needs 60% to pass; a UNF poll shows 50% yes, 35% no. It would raise the non-school homestead exemption. It does not abolish property tax.

Realtor.com's Market Clock now rates the Tampa and North Port-Bradenton-Sarasota metros as buyer's markets. In Tampa, more than 1 in 4 listings has cut its price, while rates ease to 7.32%.

Insurance & Taxes

What changes the real cost of owning

Citizens

Homeowners multiperil rates are down an average 8.8% in 2026; wind-only about 5%. Policies fell to 266,093 (Aug 28) from a 1.41M peak. Citizens resumed writing policies Oct 10.

Condo Associations

Citizens condo association rates are still rising: about +7.7% multiperil and +14.1% wind-only. Hurricane Isaias hit the Panhandle; AM Best calls it an "earnings event," not a pricing surge.

Flood Zone Note

Homeowners policies generally don't cover flood. Check the property's flood zone and get a flood quote before you write an offer, even outside required zones.

Amendment 3 · Nov 3

Raises the non-school homestead exemption to $150K in 2027 and $250K in 2028, then indexes it to inflation. It does not abolish property tax. Needs 60%. Supporters cite savings; opponents cite local budget cuts.

Condo Reserves

Since Oct 4, DBPR rule 61B-22.005 requires associations to fund reserves according to their latest structural integrity reserve study (SIRS). A clarifying amendment was proposed Oct 22.

CDD Costs

One Parrish lot type (Wildleaf 45', North River Ranch) went from $2,351 in FY2024 to $2,882 in FY2027, about +23%. Check the district's adopted schedule, not the brochure.
Sources: Citizens Property Insurance (citizensfla.com); C&S blog, Oct 2; Insurance Journal, Oct 19; WESH, Oct 14; Florida Politics, Oct 21; DBPR rule 61B-22.005 (flrules.org); Roetzel & Andress, Oct 19; North River Ranch ISD adopted assessments (verified Oct 18). Flood note is general guidance. Accessed Oct 22, 2026.

Dollars & Cents

Payment strategy still matters

Rates We Saw

Freddie Mac PMMS (Oct 22): 30-year fixed 7.32%, 15-year 6.68%. Mortgage News Daily's daily index hit 7.30%. PMMS is a weekly average; MND is a daily top-tier rate.

Incentives Worth Noting

Rate buy-downs, closing-cost help and builder incentives. Ask what each one costs, whether it is temporary, and what the payment is after it ends.

Buying Strategy

With more than 1 in 4 Tampa listings cutting price, buyers can negotiate price, credits and timing. Compare APR, points and closing costs, not just the rate.

Selling Strategy

Price right from day one. Homes in Tampa sit about 76 days; a well-presented, well-priced listing avoids chasing the market down.

What Was Approved

On Oct 20, North Port's City Commission voted 4-1 to approve the Village HL master concept plan: up to 1,875 homes on about 743 acres off South River Road near the Myakka River, plus 400,000 sq ft of retail and 100,000 sq ft of medical and office space. Residents raised environmental concerns.

Winchester Ranch: Not Approved

On Oct 10 the commission voted 3-2 only to be willing to hear an application for Winchester Ranch (about 3,000 acres, around 6,500 homes, two hospital campuses). Nothing has been approved.

Why It Matters

More new-construction supply in a metro already rated a buyer's market. Buyers get choices; resale sellers compete with builders.
Sources: yoursun.com, Oct 22 and Oct 10; DocketCity, North Port Res. No. 2026-R-68 (passed Oct 20). Accessed Oct 22, 2026.
