Market Update

September showed a split market. Single-family prices rose in Sarasota (+7.8%), Pinellas (+9.4%) and Manatee (+3.2%) and dipped slightly in Hillsborough (−1.3%). In Development

A highway, a ballpark and Wellen Park

I-75 at Fruitville Road

FDOT is rebuilding Exit 210 as a diverging diamond and widening about 2 miles of I-75 to six lanes ($177.4M). Completion is expected in 2029. Plan around overnight lane and ramp closures.

Rays Ballpark District

The Tampa Bay Rays plan a new fixed-roof ballpark, opening in 2029, as the centerpiece of a mixed-use district at Hillsborough College’s Dale Mabry campus, across from Raymond James Stadium. Hillsborough County approved the funding agreement Aug. 28, 2026, and Tampa City Council took a further financing step on Oct. 15. More approvals are still ahead. The Rays play at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg through 2028.

Wellen Park · The 743-Acre Vote

On Oct 20, 2026, North Port's City Commission approved the Wellen Park Village HL master concept plan, with conditions, on about 743 acres south of US-41 and east of South River Road, near the Myakka River. It allows up to 1,875 homes plus 400,000 sq ft of retail and services and 100,000 sq ft of professional and medical office space. The vote was 4-1; the dissenting commissioner raised environmental concerns. What it means: Wellen Park's North Port side is set to keep growing, with more homes, shops and offices planned.

Social Minute

Manatee and Sarasota Farmers Markets

A Quick Gulf Coast Reset

Farmers markets are one of the easiest ways to understand a place. You see small businesses, local products, seasonal produce and the everyday rhythm of a community.

As the region has grown, so have the options for weekend markets, independent businesses and community events.

Pick one, go early and let the morning tell you something about the area.

Dollars & Cents

As of Oct 22, 2026, the national 30-year fixed average is 7.32%, up from 6.52% in June. On a $500,000 loan that is about $268 a month more in principal and interest. The headline rate is only one part of the deal: APR, points, loan type and adjustment risk also matter.

Rates We See

Freddie Mac, Oct 22, 2026: 30-year fixed 7.32%, 15-year 6.68%. Principal and interest at 7.32%: $3,435 a month on $500,000; $2,748 on $400,000. Builders, including Pulte at Spencer Glen, advertise below-market and ARM financing; ask for the rate in writing, what it costs and how an ARM adjusts.

Selling Strategy

Private sellers may face challenges competing with builders as they are better prepared and financially designed to handle various market conditions. Sellers need to use all the tools available to attract a wider audience of buyers. Consider offering closing cost assistance, rates buy downs and accommodating terms.

Buying Strategy

Compare price, loan type, APR, points, closing costs, mortgage insurance, future adjustment risk, HOA/CDD fees and resale fit, not just the monthly teaser.

It’s 5 O’Clock Somewhere!

Florence & The Spice Boys

A food truck that started in 2019 now serves modern Middle Eastern street food at UTC, and its happy hour is one of the better deals in town.

Florence & The Spice Boys began as a food truck in 2019. Today the UTC location, in the West District, serves modern Middle Eastern street food with an indoor-outdoor bar.

Must-try: the chicken shawarma dish. The turmeric fried chicken sandwich and the falafel pita taco are close behind, and Florence's desserts, like the Goldfish sugar cookie, are worth saving room for.

Happy hour is the easy way in: daily from 3 to 5. Stay from 5 to 6, order off the regular menu, and martinis are 99 cents.

Community Focus

Spencer Glen at Riverview

Spencer Glen · The Buzz

A new Pulte Homes community in southern Riverview, about a mile from the I-75/Big Bend Road interchange and US-301, with single-family and multigenerational "AllGen" plans and a resort-style amenity center at its heart.

Spencer Glen · Highlights

Homes of 1,662 to 4,272 sq ft. AllGen plans add a private suite with living area, bedroom, bath and optional kitchenette. Builder base prices from $400,990 (Oct 22, 2026; changes often, excludes lot premiums and options; not a sale price). Models at 13716 Sunlight Meadow Dr.

Spencer Glen · Amenities

Clubhouse · zero-entry resort-style pool with private cabanas · fitness center and screened game room · pickleball · playground and dog park · walking trails.

What Makes Spencer Glen Different

Southern Riverview, nestled next to I-75 with quick access to Tampa and beyond: about 20 miles to downtown Tampa and 45 miles to Sarasota. HOA about $91/month and CDD about $2,194-$2,921/year.

Insurance & Storms

Weather, storms and the cost of cover

Hurricane Season

NOAA's updated 2026 Atlantic hurricane outlook (Aug 6) gives a 75% chance of a below-normal season, a 20% chance of near normal and a 5% chance of above-normal activity.

The Climate

January highs are in the low 70s; June to September highs run near 91°F. About 60% of the roughly 49 inches of yearly rain falls June to September, mostly in afternoon storms. Hurricane season runs June 1 to Nov 30.

Flood Is Separate

Standard homeowners policies don't cover flood. New flood policies usually have a 30-day wait, except at a purchase with a mortgage.

Low Risk Isn't No Risk

Nearly 1 in 4 flood insurance claims are paid on policies in low-to-moderate-risk zones (X and X500), per the City of Sarasota.

Insurance Averages

Homeowners premium incl. wind, all policies in force as of Mar 31, 2026 (not a quote): Hillsborough $3,525 · Pinellas $4,063 · Sarasota $3,457 · Manatee $3,181 a year.

Prep Basics

Check the parcel on FEMA's flood map and ask for the wind mitigation report.
